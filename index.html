String Mirror

Created by: Hashem Al-Ghaili

Stretch strings in the air, give each one an instrument, and play them with your fingers.

  1. 1Stretch. Pinch thumb to index finger on both hands, pull your hands apart, then let go. A string stays where you left it.
  2. 2Choose. A palette of 24 instruments opens on the new string. Point at one and hold still (or pinch it), then pick its tone: major, minor and more.
  3. 3Play. Sweep a fingertip across a string. Where you cross it decides the note. Add a beat, loop your own layers, and record the result.

Use Chrome or Edge. The hand tracker loads from the internet the first time. Sit 40 to 80 cm from the camera with light on your hands. A webcam is required — this build has no mouse mode.

How to play

Strings

  • Make a string: pinch with both hands, pull apart, let go.
  • Pluck it: sweep an open fingertip across it.
  • Hold it: rest a fingertip on the string to sustain.
  • Bend and snap: pinch a string and pull it sideways.
  • Change it: pinch the badge in the middle of a string.

Pointing and pressing

  • The amber ring between your thumb and index finger is your pointer.
  • Menus: point at a choice and hold still for a moment, or pinch it.
  • Buttons: put the amber ring on any button and do a quick pinch.

Keys: Space plays or stops the beat, R records, F films, L records a layer, Z removes the last layer, H hides or shows the controls, Esc closes menus.
